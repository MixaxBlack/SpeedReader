# SpeedReader design
Native Android, Operate / Read. Quiet paper-colored reading surface, forest primary controls, generous central word stage. Material buttons, sliders, radio choices, navigation Back and inset-aware layouts. Typography in sp and 48dp touch targets. Default reading word 42sp serif; UI Roboto. Warm Paper default; Night, Sepia, Ink, Forest, Ocean, Plum, Sand, Slate and High Contrast presets. Custom colors affect the reading stage only so controls remain legible. All user-facing UI text has English and Russian equivalents.

The controls explicitly use the Material Light theme in both system modes; reading themes remain independently selectable. Custom reading colors require at least 4.5:1 contrast, including the smaller reading-status text.

Speed uses a 64dp-wide vertical native SeekBar rail beside the reading stage, with 500 at the top and 30 at the bottom. The rail fills available reading height; compact-height layouts allocate a scrollable 420dp reading area. Native accessibility range and keyboard control are retained. A row beneath the stage places +1/−1 buttons around the current speed, preserving a long track and single-line step labels. Position seeking remains horizontal.

The library is a full native screen with a top bar, Find FB2 / Scan again controls, a search field and a flat ListView. Book rows lead with the embedded title and author, followed by series/language/word count/progress and genre. Metadata is never invented. Folder access uses Android's document-tree picker. Live scan totals and Stop scan share the screen; unavailable or corrupt files are summarized without interrupting the scan. On compact-height screens, help copy is omitted and the status uses two lines to preserve visible book rows. The system Back gesture returns to reading and stops an active scan, preserving imported books.
