# Reader, library and appearance

## Direction contract

THESIS: A quiet personal reading desk. One word dominates the reader; title and author dominate the library. Controls have clear priorities and never compete with the book.

OWN-WORLD: Warm bone #F7F6F3, charcoal #252922, muted olive #62685D, fine #DCDDD5 rules and restrained forest #426650. Manrope with Cyrillic for controls, native serif for book titles. Flat six-dp buttons, eight-dp fields, no shadows or ornamental imagery.

STORY: Open a book, scan a chosen folder or scan accessible phone storage, recognize the book from its metadata or source filename, then read with immediate pause and precise speed control. Phone-wide access is optional; the folder flow remains available without it. Appearance groups language, preview, typography, themes, custom colors and the vertical reading-speed slider at the bottom.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Serif screen heading beside a quiet secondary action. Reader has book title, import/library controls, a compact native-vector rotation button and a full-width word stage, followed by speed steps, position and transport. The underlined speed value opens Appearance at its slider. Each word fits independently within the stage and returns to the preferred size when it fits. Library has a prominent Scan phone action, Found files / Books switch, folder actions, search, status and plain editorial rows. Compact screens move secondary actions into More. Found-file rows lead with title or filename, then author and path, and open by tapping.

FORM: Refinement of the incumbent native reader and flat library list. No layout lottery is needed for the existing functional composition. Native ripples and focus feedback; no animation on words, seeking or repeated speed actions. Ten theme previews use two columns.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
