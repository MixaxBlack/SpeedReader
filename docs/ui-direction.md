# Reader, library and appearance

## Direction contract

THESIS: A quiet personal reading desk. One word dominates the reader; title and author dominate the library. Controls have clear priorities and never compete with the book.

OWN-WORLD: Warm bone #F7F6F3, charcoal #252922, muted olive #62685D, fine #DCDDD5 rules and restrained forest #426650. Manrope with Cyrillic for controls, native serif for book titles. Flat six-dp buttons, eight-dp fields, no shadows or ornamental imagery.

STORY: Open a book or scan a folder, recognize the book from its metadata, then read with immediate pause and precise speed control. Appearance groups language, preview, typography, themes and custom colors.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Serif screen heading beside a quiet secondary action. Reader has book title, import/library controls, a large word stage and tall speed rail, followed by position and transport. Library has a prominent scan action, search, status and plain editorial rows.

FORM: Refinement of the incumbent native reader and flat library list. No layout lottery is needed for the existing functional composition. Native ripples and focus feedback; no animation on words, seeking or repeated speed actions. Ten theme previews use two columns.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
